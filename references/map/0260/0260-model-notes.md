## Placement and orientation

The configuration intentionally uses the workbook point `[14.42232, 35.94888]` rather than specifying a separate model centre. Its bearing is 155°, altitude 0, scale 1 and minimum zoom 14. The entrance is near local X=0 and faces local -X: heading 115° (ESE), toward Dawret il-Qawra.

Unlike models centred on their nave, most of this building extends behind the anchor in local +X. The lower body is 18 × 12 m with a 4.2 m ground-floor volume; upper floors and a rooftop room raise the total to 21.05 m. Including the entrance projections, the unrotated model spans approximately 19.75 × 12.5 m.

## Architectural interpretation

The chapel occupies the ground-floor frontage of a larger building. The model therefore includes the limestone entrance, white-framed doors and glazing, a simplified sign, entrance paving, upper residential structure with three glazed balcony levels, parapet and rooftop room. It is not represented as a detached church with a tower or dome.

Dimensions, colours and floor heights describe the existing visual approximation, not surveyed measurements. The OSM context was added retrospectively to record nearby buildings and roads. It does not independently validate the entrance anchor or all footprint edges, and no model geometry or placement was changed during this reference completion.
