## Architectural interpretation

The existing geometry represents weathered limestone walls, pale corner trim, flat plaster roofs, the doorway and facade decoration, side windows and spouts, a multi-opening bell gable with bells and cross, and a low balustraded/walled forecourt.

These dimensions describe the implemented approximation. Heights and decorative shapes are estimates from the supplied photographs.
