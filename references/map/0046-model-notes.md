## Placement and orientation

The model uses `[14.56411, 35.86592]`, `bearing: 334`, altitude 0, scale 1 and minimum zoom 14. Its local X nave axis runs approximately ESE–WNW. The decorated entrance elevation and statue are on the local -Z side; under this transform that side faces heading 26° (NNE). It is not the blank local -X nave end.

The nave's lower body is 44 × 16 m with 10.5 m walls; the raised portion is 34 × 12.4 m. A transverse volume and a tall square bell tower extend the plan. The unrotated model spans approximately 51.12 × 25.32 m including decorative projections, and reaches 52.73 m at the tower cross.

## Architectural interpretation

The model includes warm pink limestone walls, flat stepped roof levels, pale cornices and corbels, clerestory openings, a decorated transverse elevation with rose windows and statue, and a tall rear-side bell tower with paired openings, a pyramidal cap and cross.

Heights and facade detail are interpretations of the supplied images. The new context export preserves current map geometry and nearby roads; it does not reproduce the exact historical OSM response used during alignment. Existing model geometry and placement were retained while completing these reference files.
