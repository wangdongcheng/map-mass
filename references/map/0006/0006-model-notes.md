## Placement and orientation

The existing model is anchored at `[14.51262, 35.8977]`, with `bearing: 37`, scale 1, altitude 0 and minimum zoom 14. Local -X is the main entrance; the map transform places it at heading 233° (SW). The current bearing includes the later orientation adjustment recorded in Git history.

The central nave body is 59 m long, 20 m wide and 14 m high. Side chapels, the wider facade and twin towers extend beyond that rectangle. The full unrotated model, including ornament and steps, spans approximately 64.63 × 38.95 m in plan and reaches 34.55 m high.

## Architectural interpretation

The model contains the limestone nave, pitched roof, repeated side chapels and clerestory windows, a restrained central facade with pilasters and portal, twin bell towers, a clock, roof finials and crosses, and low entrance steps. Materials and decorative details are simplified for the map renderer.

These dimensions describe the existing geometry; they are not survey measurements. The OSM context was added retrospectively and does not establish that every edge of the older model matches the current footprint. No geometry or placement was changed while completing these reference files. Interiors and neighbouring buildings remain outside the custom model.
