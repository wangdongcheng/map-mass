## Placement and orientation

The existing anchor is `[14.48774, 35.88232]`, with `bearing: 14`, altitude 0, scale 1 and minimum zoom 14. The entrance lies at local -X and faces heading 256° (WSW) after the map transform.

The main body is 43.5 × 18 m, with 12.2 m side walls. A lower annex projects from the local +Z side. The full unrotated model, including that annex, facade and steps, spans approximately 48.03 × 26.70 m and reaches 21 m high.

## Architectural interpretation

The model captures the segmented barrel roof and ribs, broad decorated facade, rose window, main portal and additional doors, side buttresses and arched windows, low annex and broad entrance steps. Limestone, roof and glass colours follow the existing simplified material palette.

The dimensions above are measured from the implemented geometry, not from a survey. The context export was added after modelling and is provided for comparison with the surrounding map. No exact footprint-fit claim is made for this older model. Its geometry, anchor and orientation were retained when completing these files.
